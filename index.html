Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

NEXGEN ACADEMY ("we", "our", or "us") operates the NEXGEN ACADEMY mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide secure access to our educational courses, live classes, and study materials, we use Firebase Authentication. Through this service, we may collect:

  • Phone Number: Collected via SMS-based phone verification for account creation and secure login.
  • Name / Profile Information: Collected to manage student accounts within the app.

2. Use of Data

We use the collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our educational services.
  • To authenticate user identity and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To manage student support and account notifications.

3. Data Security and Transmission

All data transmitted from the app to our servers and Firebase backend is encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS protocols. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Account and Data Deletion Requests (NEXGEN ACADEMY)

Users can request the permanent deletion of their NEXGEN ACADEMY account and associated data at any time by following these steps:

  1. Send an email to support@nexgendoctorsacademy.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include your registered Phone Number and Full Name in the email so we can verify and locate your account.

Data Deleted & Retention Policy: Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your account, profile name, and phone number from our Firebase Authentication and database records within 7 business days. No personal data is retained after deletion unless required by law for financial/tax compliance.
